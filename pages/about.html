Our Mission: Curating the Cosmos for Every Explorer

STELLARIUM is an open-access digital astronomy museum designed to transform raw astronomical data, deep-space telescope imagery, and human spaceflight milestones into an interactive architectural gallery.

✦ Architectural Storytelling

Organized into a Grand Rotunda and four specialized exhibition wings spanning 18 curated rooms, guiding visitors from our own Sun to the edge of observable space.

✦ Multi-Wavelength Discovery

Bridges optical, infrared, and X-ray observations from flagship observatories so visitors can compare how modern instruments reveal hidden cosmic structures.

✦ Interactive Learning

Combines visual comparisons, chronological timelines, trivia assessments, and bookmarkable visitor passes to make space science engaging and accessible.

NASA & Observatory Image Credits

All astronomical photography, deep-field composites, and mission telemetry featured across Stellarium are credited to public-domain space science archives.

NASA / ESA / CSA — James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)

Near-infrared and mid-infrared deep-field captures, including Stephan's Quintet, Carina Nebula cliffs, and multi-observatory galactic composites.

NASA / ESA — Hubble Space Telescope & Chandra X-Ray

Optical and high-energy X-ray imagery of the Andromeda Galaxy (M31), Pillars of Creation, Orion Nebula, and Hubble Birthday archive observations.

NASA / JPL-Caltech & Planetary Missions

Planetary portraits and surface panoramas from Cassini-Huygens, Juno, Voyager 1 & 2, Sojourner, Curiosity, and the Perseverance Mars rover.

NASA Johnson Space Center & Event Horizon Telescope

Apollo lunar surface photography, International Space Station expedition imagery, and the Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) M87* black hole shadow observation.
