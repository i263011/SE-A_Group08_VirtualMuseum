From Expendable to Rapid Reuse

Breakthroughs Across Four Eras

Standing 110.6 m tall, Saturn V burned 20 metric tons of propellant per second across five F-1 engines to send 48,600 kg to the Moon—and remains 100% successful across 13 launches.
The Shuttle introduced reusable RS-25 main engines and winged runway landings, assembling the International Space Station and servicing the Hubble Space Telescope in orbit.
By guiding its first stage back with supersonic retropropulsion and grid fins onto drone ships, Falcon 9 transformed orbital launch cadence and slashed per-kilogram cost to orbit.
At 121 m tall with 33 methalox Raptor engines and mechanical tower catch arms, Starship is engineered to return both booster and ship for rapid turnaround and interplanetary cargo.
